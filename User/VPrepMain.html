Frequently Asked questions


How do I find details about a specific course?
Simply click on the corresponding course button under the “Courses” section. The relevant course details will be displayed, and the page will scroll to the detailed information section.
What types of courses are offered?
The courses encompass all the mandatory core subjects and electives listed in your official curriculum, ensuring that students have access to the complete range of academic content needed to fulfill degree requirements.
Can I access past year question papers and notes for these courses?
Yes! Alongside each course, you will find resources such as notes, important topics, and previous years’ question papers to help with your preparation.
Is this site updated regularly with new course information?
Yes, I regularly update the course listings and resources each semester to keep the information current and useful.
Who can I contact for additional academic support or suggestions?
You can reach me out through “Contact Us” section for further guidance or feedback.
Do I need an account to view materials?
Yes. You need to sign up and log in to access study materials. This helps keep your data secure and lets me personalize your experience.
What if my course or subject is missing?
If you don’t find your course, you can contact me through the “Contact” section or social links. I’ll try to add that subject and its resources as soon as possible.
Is this build by VIT officially?
No,This initiative is a community-driven effort and is not officially affiliated with VIT. It is a personal endeavor undertaken with the aim of enhancing the campus experience for everyone.If you have found this platform beneficial, sharing it with your peers would be the most valuable form of support.
Is there a mobile-friendly version of this site?
Yes, the site is optimized for all devices including smartphones and tablets for easy access anytime, anywhere.
